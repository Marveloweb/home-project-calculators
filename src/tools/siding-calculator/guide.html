How the siding calculator works

Siding is estimated by wall area and sold by the square: 100 sq ft of wall. The calculator follows CertainTeed's siding estimating form. Walls are measured from the foundation to the eaves with doors and windows left in, gables are added as triangles with an extra foot of height for the angled cuts, and only half of any garage or sliding door comes off. Then 10% is added for waste, the figure CertainTeed and the Journal of Light Construction's vinyl siding field guide both use.

Walls = distance around the house × wall height
Gables = number × ½ × gable width × (gable height + 1 ft)
Siding area = walls + gables − ½ × garage and sliding doors
Squares = siding area × (1 + 10%) ÷ 100
Vinyl cartons = squares ÷ 2
Fiber cement planks = squares × 100 ÷ exposure in inches

Why leave windows in? Panels are cut around every opening, and the pieces you cut out are rarely long enough to use anywhere else. Taking the windows off would leave you short.

Worked example

A 40 by 30 ft house is 140 ft around. With 9 ft walls that is 140 × 9 = 1,260 sq ft. It has two gables 30 ft wide and 6 ft high: 2 × ½ × 30 × 7 = 210 sq ft. The total is 1,470 sq ft, or 1,617 sq ft with 10% extra: 16.2 squares.

  • Vinyl: 16.2 ÷ 2 = 9 cartons of 2 squares.
  • Fiber cement at a 7 in exposure: 16.17 × 100 ÷ 7 = 231 planks 12 ft long.
  • Starter strip: 140 ft × 1.1 ÷ 12 = 13 pieces.
  • J-channel: 16 ft around each of 12 windows, 17 ft around each of 2 doors, 64.6 ft up the gable rakes and 80 ft under the eaves (140 − 2 × 30) is 371 ft, or 34 pieces of 12 ft with 10% extra.
  • Corners: four 10 ft outside corner posts, one per corner.
  • House wrap: 1,470 sq ft plus 10% for laps is 2 rolls of 9 by 150 ft.

Trim and accessories

For vinyl, the calculator counts:

  • Starter strip along the bottom of every wall, in 12 ft pieces.
  • J-channel around windows and doors, under the eaves and up the gable rakes, the places CertainTeed lists. A 3 by 5 ft window takes 16 ft and a 3 by 7 ft door 17 ft (no channel across the sill).
  • Corner posts, 10 ft long, one for each outside and inside corner, two where walls are taller than 10 ft.

For fiber cement, corners and openings are framed with 12 ft trim boards (two boards on each outside corner), and the starter strip is ripped from extra planks. As a check against the J-channel count, the Journal of Light Construction suggests about 200 linear ft of J-trim and 100 ft of starter per 1,000 sq ft of vinyl siding.

Before you buy

  • Measure every wall, including bump-outs and the sides of dormers, and count the corners you walk past.
  • Order all the siding at once, from one production run, so the color matches. Vinyl colors from different runs can differ slightly.
  • Vinyl expands about 5/8 in over 12 ft between winter and summer. Leave 1/4 in gaps at posts and channels (3/8 in when it is below 40°F) and nail in the center of the slots, never tight.
  • Soffit, fascia, and window and door flashing tape are separate items; measure the eaves for soffit if you are replacing it.
